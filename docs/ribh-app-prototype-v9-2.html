    Qard e Hasanah

    The beautiful loan

    Interest-free lending to ease a brother or sister's hardship is coming to Ribh. We are building it carefully, with the board.

    Coming soon

    1What it is

    Qard al-Hasan is a benevolent loan: you lend, and the borrower repays exactly what was lent, no more. Any extra demanded would be riba. The reward is with Allah, not in the repayment.

    2Why it matters

    It is the only loan the Quran calls beautiful, and it is described as a loan to Allah Himself, multiplied in reward. It protects people from interest-bearing debt at their most vulnerable moments.

    3How it will work on Ribh

    A dedicated fund, separate from your investments, with clear eligibility, dignified repayment terms, and full transparency about where the money goes. The structure is under design and review by our Shariah board now.

    "Who is it that will lend to Allah a goodly loan so that He may multiply it for him many times over?"Al-Baqarah 2:245 · rendering pending board review
