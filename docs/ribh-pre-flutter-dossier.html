Ribh Investments · pre-Flutter architecture dossier

Before we build in Flutter

A two-phase evaluation of the v9.2 prototype, prepared before committing the codebase to Flutter. Phase 1 hardens feature integration and removes UX friction. Phase 2 renders the whole system visually: screen map, entity relationships, the core activity, and the money flow. The goal is a single source of truth a developer can build from with no ambiguity, and a clear-eyed answer to one question: can this be a masterclass app?

Base: ribh-app-prototype-v9.2 Target stack: Flutter + Dart, Supabase backend Status: evaluation only, no build this session

1.3The richer Barakah, from fintech and Islamic-app research

Mature Islamic apps (Muslim Pro, Zoya, and the Wahed faith layer) compound engagement through daily worship habit, learning, and gentle streaks, never through more financial numbers. Six blocks, all reusing v8 and v9 components, no new visual language.

BlockWhat it doesReuses

Guardrail: everything here is habit and knowledge, never a leaderboard or a public display of worship. All verse, hadith, and adhkar text is board-reviewed before launch. The score explicitly states it never measures worship itself.

1.4Structural decisions this forces

Two calls to make before the Flutter build starts:
  • The Grow tab is now thin. After dedupe it holds the Ribh Fund, auto-invest, and goal editing. Recommendation: keep Grow as the "growth engine" tab (Fund plus auto-invest plus goals), which is a coherent identity, rather than folding it. Five tabs stay.
  • Savings discretion, still unresolved in product terms. The UI now routes Savings through the approval queue, which is correct, but if you ever want true hands-off investing you need a signed discretionary Wakalah mandate and a licence. Decide before launch, not before build.

Why this can be a masterclass app, honestly argued:
  • Trust is designed in, not bolted on. The amanah tracker, the open recovery view, and per-deal consent answer the three fears a first-time halal investor has. No competitor in the benchmark does all three.
  • One mental model, everywhere. After Phase 1 dedupe, each capability has exactly one home. Users never meet two versions of the same thing, and developers never build it twice.
  • Faith and finance reinforce each other. The enriched Barakah and Learn turn a transactional app into a daily habit, which is precisely how the most successful Islamic apps retain users.
  • The books cannot lie. A single append-only ledger invariant underpins every money movement, which is both a Shariah virtue and an audit-grade engineering foundation.
  • Accessible and honest by construction. AA contrast in both themes, 44px targets, risk disclosure wherever returns appear, and no dark patterns.
What must still be true for the masterclass claim to hold:
  • Regulatory registration (BSEC, Bangladesh Bank) completed before real funds move.
  • The named Shariah board reviews every faith-facing string, and the two placeholder scholars become real, consenting members.
  • Bengali localisation extended to the new v9 content, not just v8.
  • The Savings discretion question answered with a signed Wakalah mandate or kept in the approval queue.
  • The moderated usability sessions from the UX audit actually run, so real users, not this document, have the last word.

Verdict: the architecture is masterclass-capable. Whether the app becomes one depends on execution discipline (holding the one-owner rule and the ledger invariant through the Flutter build) and on the five conditions above, which are business and compliance work, not design. Nothing in the structure blocks a top-tier CX rating.
