What changed from v0.7 to v8.0

  • One new local store (preferences) and five new production tables (shariah_board, deposit_transactions, autosave_schedule, nominees, referrals).
  • One new ledger kind (purification) on the existing append-only wallet_transactions.
  • Deposits moved from a single simulated flow to a three-provider rail (bKash, Nagad, bank) with name-match.
  • Me tab restructured into a settings cluster plus the Shariah board; sheet layer grew from ~30 to 40+.
  • A first-run onboarding tour now sits above the tab bar for new users.
